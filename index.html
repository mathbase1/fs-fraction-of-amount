Error clinicReasoning2 min

Which method is wrong?

Repair the reasoning before you reveal the answer.

CLAIM A
“To find 35 of 40, I do 40 ÷ 3 × 5.”
Wrong order. Divide by the denominator: 40 ÷ 5 × 3 = 24.
CLAIM B
“14 of 28 is 7, so 34 of 28 is 21.”
Correct. One quarter is 7; three quarters is 21.
CLAIM C
“56 of 42 = 42 ÷ 6 = 7.”
Incomplete. 7 is one sixth. Multiply by 5 → 35.
